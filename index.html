01 · Problem & effect

Trust is tested at the matching moment.

Your screenshots show that the assigned-captain screen already includes captain and vehicle details, an “Always on time” badge, and a “Check” option for a better captain nearby.

The gap we are testingPassengers may still lack a clear, deliberate moment to review the available reliability information before deciding to continue.
1
Potential effect: hesitationThe passenger has to interpret profile information while a pickup is already underway.
2
Potential effect: cancellation or rematchUncertainty could cause avoidable drop-off, but this effect needs user research to confirm.

Evidence boundary: screenshots establish the current interface, not how widespread this concern is. A sample of 150 reviews included 6 ride-acceptance mentions, not 6 explicit requests for this feature.

02 · Solution & impact

Captain Trust Check

After a captain accepts, add a review card to the existing assigned-captain screen before the passenger proceeds.

✓
Show useful contextRating and rating count, completed rides, tenure, existing badges, and verification status where available.
✓
Make the choice explicitContinue with Captain, or Find Another Captain with the expected waiting-time trade-off.
✓
Be fair to new captainsLabel “New captain”; hide unreliable statistics instead of treating low trip counts as a safety warning.
Intended impactMore informed decisions and potentially fewer captain-related cancellations.
What must be provenConfidence improves without unacceptable pickup delays or unfair captain selection.

Captain metrics and verification statuses in this prototype are fictional sample data, not live Rapido records.
